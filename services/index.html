Bentonite Clay Mud Mask (30 min)

$45

This treatment includes gentle massage of all four legs with a bentonite clay mask followed by a moisturizing MSF herbal salve application.

Bentonite clay is renowned for its many benefits to humans and horses alike. Loaded with natural minerals, it is known to reduce inflammation, increase circulation, draw out heat and excess fluids and toxins, while having a cooling effect for tendons and joints.

Wonderful as preventative maintenance or before or after exercise.
